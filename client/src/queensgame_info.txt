Queens is a logic puzzle, not a chess game. On an 8×8 grid, your goal is to place 8 queens so that none of them conflicts with another. The classic version requires no two queens to share a row, column, or diagonal; the popular app-style version also adds colored regions.

Modern Queens Game

The modern version divides the 8×8 board into 8 colored regions. You must place:

    One queen in every row.

    One queen in every column.

    One queen in every colored region.

    No two queens diagonally adjacent to each other.

So a queen blocks its whole row and column, but only the four immediately touching diagonal squares—not the entire diagonal line.
