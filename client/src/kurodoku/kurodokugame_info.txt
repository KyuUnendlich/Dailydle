Kurodoku (Kuromasu) is a binary-determination logic puzzle published by Nikoli.

Rules
-----
1. Fill cells black or white according to these rules:
   - Each number represents the count of white cells visible from that cell (including itself)
   - Numbered cells must always be white
   - No two black cells may be horizontally or vertically adjacent
   - All white cells must be connected horizontally or vertically

2. A cell is visible from another cell if:
   - Both are in the same row or column
   - There are no black cells between them in that row/column

3. Each puzzle has exactly one unique solution.

Notation
--------
- Black cells: filled/shaded
- White cells: empty/unshaded
- Numbered cells: always white (never black)
